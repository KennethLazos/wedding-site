FAQs

Anything else? Message us directly and we'll help.

When should I arrive?

Please arrive at The Manila Cathedral by 12:30 PM. The Mass begins promptly at 1:00 PM.

Is there parking?

Limited parking is available around Intramuros. We recommend carpooling or booking a ride. Palacio de Manila has parking for reception guests.

Can I bring a plus-one?

We've reserved seats for the guests named on your invitation. Please enter your number of guests in the RSVP form.

Are children invited?

We love your little ones, but apart from those in the entourage, this will be an adults-only celebration.

Can I take photos during the ceremony?

We're having an unplugged ceremony. Please keep phones and cameras away during Mass so our photographers can capture every moment. Snap away at the reception!

When is the RSVP deadline?
