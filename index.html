Our Story

Every love story has a beginning. Ours began with a simple hello.

2018

Where It All Began

We first met at the office in 2018. What started as simple jokes, playful teasing, and casual conversations slowly turned into something more. Neither of us knew where those little moments would lead, but somehow, we found ourselves looking forward to seeing each other a little more each day. Elena made the first move and pursued John for almost a year. Yes, you read that right—almost a year! Looking back, who would have thought that a simple office connection would eventually turn into a lifetime kind of love?

2019

The Beginning of Us

After almost a year of pursuing John, Elena was honestly getting tired and was close to giving up. But just when she thought their story might not go anywhere, John began to realize that he felt the same way. And finally, on August 3, 2019, we officially became a couple. As a little plot twist, John was actually the first to say, “I love you.” Sometimes, love takes a little time to find its way, but when it does, everything falls into place.

2020

Learning to Do Life Together

Then came 2020, and the world suddenly slowed down. During the pandemic, we decided to live together. It was a different kind of adventure—one that allowed us to see each other beyond the dates and special occasions. We shared the happy days, the stressful moments, the ordinary routines, and even the little disagreements. We learned that love wasn’t always about grand gestures or exciting moments. Sometimes, it was simply about having someone beside you, making even the most ordinary days feel special. And that was when we realized how much we loved doing life together.

2022–2025

Making Memories Along the Way

As the years passed, we found more reasons to explore, travel, and make memories together. From Zambales to Cebu, Davao, Siargao, Bicol, Legazpi, and many other places, we enjoyed discovering new destinations and experiencing little adventures along the way. Some trips were carefully planned, while others happened spontaneously. We collected photographs, stories, laughter, and memories that will always have a special place in our hearts. But wherever we went, we learned that the best part was never just the destination—it was having each other along for the journey.

2026

The Question That Led to Forever

And then came the moment we will always hold close to our hearts. During the In Between concert of TJ Monterde and KZ Tandingan, John surprised Elena with a question that would change our story forever. After all the years of growing together, choosing each other, and experiencing everything in between, John was ready to take the next step.

And Elena said yes.

After all, how could she say no to the man she once spent almost a year pursuing? This time, there was no waiting, no guessing, and no more wondering what the future held. Just two hearts, ready to promise each other a lifetime.

And now, our forever begins.

Looking back, we realize that our story was never about having a perfect beginning or a perfect journey. It was about two people who found each other unexpectedly, grew together through the years, and continued choosing one another through every season of life.

From those simple office conversations in 2018 to the promise of forever in 2026, every little moment brought us exactly where we are today.

And now, as we prepare to write another chapter, we look forward to more laughter, more adventures, more ordinary days made extraordinary, and a lifetime of making memories together.

Because our favorite part of the story has always been—and will always be—having each other.
